Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore my premium subscription?

Open the app, go to Settings > Premium, and tap "Restore Purchases" at the bottom of the screen.

Where are my backups stored?

Backups are stored securely in your personal Google Drive in a hidden app-data folder accessible only to TopHabits.

Can I track more than 3 goals?

Yes! You can track unlimited goals with a Premium subscription, but you can "pin" up to 3 to your main dashboard for maximum focus.
